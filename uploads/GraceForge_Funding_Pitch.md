## The Problem

### Most discipleship tools stop at inspiration.

Christians have access to more biblical content than at any point in history — sermons, podcasts, devotionals, study Bibles. Yet sanctification remains as difficult as ever. The gap is not knowledge. It is application. People know *what* Scripture says. They lack a framework for *how* to deploy it against specific, named temptations in real-time.

GraceForge was built to close that gap.

> GraceForge translates biblical principles on resisting sin into interactive mathematical resistance equations — giving believers a practical, diagnostic tool to fight *from* victory in Christ rather than *for* it.
>
> **NO DIRECT PRECEDENT IN CHRISTIAN LITERATURE**

## What's Built — Beta v10

### A complete personal discipleship engine.

The current app is a fully functional Progressive Web App (PWA) covering 12 sin categories. Four tabs organized by user intent carry the user from understanding to daily practice to recovery. Beta v10 expands the Journey tab significantly — adding a curated Worship player and a full Discipleship Journal — and introduces user-facing settings controls.

### Guide Tab

**📖 Resistance Equation Library**
12 sin categories each with a named diagnostic equation. Variables are scored 1–10. A live R-score tells the user exactly where they stand and which variable to move first.

**📜 Biblical Case Studies**
Every category includes a scored biblical figure (e.g., Saul for Envy, Joseph for Lust) showing the exact variable profile that led to sin or resistance.

**🔍 Worship Diagnosis**
Each sin is identified by its underlying idol — what it worships and what worship act replaces it. Root-cause framing, not behavior management.

**👑 Expanded Biblical Case Studies** *(v10 Update)*
Biblical figures updated and deepened across categories — including Nebuchadnezzar (Pride), with fully scored variable profiles showing exact theological failure points. Every case study now carries a complete diagnostic alongside the narrative.

### Equip Tab

**🧠 Scripture Memory Flashcards**
Category-tagged flashcard system with flip animation. Users build their own memory deck across all 12 sin areas — hiding the Word as preloaded ammunition.

**⚖️ Master Principles Framework**
Cross-category synthesizing principles with a master equation that captures the universal structure of resistance. Connects all 12 categories into a unified theology.

### Journey Tab

**📊 Resistance Progression Tracker**
Bar chart tracking R-score trajectory over time. Date-stamped journaling with category tagging and variable movement tracking — a visible record of growth.

**🕊 Weekly Sabbath Review**
Structured weekly reflection protocol — honest diagnosis of the week with God before the next one begins. Rhythm-building for the long obedience.

**🎵 Worship Atmosphere Player** *(NEW in v10)*
Embedded curated worship playlist integrated directly into the Journey tab. Prepares the heart for reflection, journaling, and prayer before the week's review — anchored in Ephesians 5:19. Armor worn through praise before the work begins.

**📝 Discipleship Journal** *(NEW in v10)*
Full date-stamped journaling system with category tagging, variable movement tracking ("God-Consciousness rose from 3 to 7"), and a dedicated prayer/reflection field. A running record of what God is doing — battles, victories, and turning points documented over time.

### Battle Tab

**🛡 Jehoshaphat Protocol**
Pre-battle worship tool: category-specific Scripture, prayer prompt, and gratitude prompt — positioned before the fight, not after. Worship leads the army.

**🔄 Post-Fall Debrief**
3-step guided diagnostic after failure — not condemnation, reconstruction. Names the trigger, scores variables at the moment of the fall, identifies what to rebuild first.

**🧪 Scenario Builder**
Real-time leverage analysis across three scenarios. Shows which single variable change produces the biggest R-score gain — the highest-leverage next step.

### Always Available

**🚨 Emergency Mode**
Floating persistent button. Four-phase overlay: category selection → breathing prayer → Scripture and prayer → action step. Designed for the moment temptation arrives.

**⚡ Overcoming & Committing Pairs** *(Parallel Scenarios)*
Every category has a same-protagonist pair — one scenario of resistance, one of surrender. Isolates exactly which variable difference changes the outcome.

**⚙ Settings & Accessibility Controls** *(App-Wide · NEW in v10)*
User-facing settings overlay with UI scale controls — making the app usable across devices and accessible to users who need larger text. Demonstrates production-level attention to real-world usability.

## The Gap

### The app's own framework identifies the next frontier.

Across all 12 sin categories, one variable consistently appears as the most destructive denominator: **Isolation.** Every recovery story in the app turns on one moment — a person ending isolation and reaching toward another believer.

GraceForge currently functions as a **personal discipleship tool that supports community**. Its next chapter is building the relational infrastructure that reduces the Isolation variable at scale — evolving into a **community platform with personal discipleship tools at its core.**

That evolution requires backend investment. Here is what the roadmap looks like.

---

## Phase 2 Vision — Community

### Four tiers of gospel-centered community.

Organized by relational tier, reflecting the biblical model of community from one-on-one covenant to church-wide discipleship infrastructure.

### Tier I — One-on-One Accountability
*The most strategic and highest-leverage tier*

- **Accountability Partner Linkup** — Two users connect via a shared room code. R-score trends are shared, prayer check-ins sent, and silence itself becomes a data point — the app knows when someone has gone dark.
- **Covenant Witnessing** — A personal covenant (e.g., Eye-Covenant) is sent to a partner for digital counter-signature. Witnessed covenants carry different weight than private ones.
- **Post-Fall Debrief Share** — After a debrief, users can send a gospel-framed summary to their partner — structured confession replacing the awkward "I need to tell you something" text.

### Tier II — Small Group
*Discipleship tools that work inside existing community structures*

- **Small Group Battle Room** — Groups of 4–12 share a space with aggregate R-score trajectories, shared category assignments, and collective Sabbath Review questions.
- **Communal Scripture Memory Board** — Group members pool memorized verses. The collective armor of the body becomes visible — and a natural discipleship conversation starter.
- **Group Scenario Builder Challenges** — A leader posts a scenario; members run it independently and compare results. Bible study and discipleship fused into one interactive exercise.

### Tier III — Church & Pastoral
*Tools that make pastors genuinely useful to their people*

- **Pastoral Dashboard** — Anonymized aggregate view of congregational spiritual health — which categories are spiking, where people are falling. Diagnostic intelligence for sermon planning.
- **Guided 40-Day Cohort** — A pastor launches a sanctification cohort. Daily assigned categories, Scripture, and reflection prompts. Individual and communal rhythm built in.
- **Prayer Request Integration** — Category-tagged prayer needs distributed to opted-in intercessors. Diagnostic work and intercession meeting in one place.

### Tier IV — Broader Community
*Anonymous, gospel-centered public space*

- **Testimony Wall** — Anonymized recovery stories structured around the Post-Fall Debrief format. Real-world equivalents of the app's named scenarios — submitted by actual users.
- **Category Prayer Rooms** — Topic-specific prayer spaces per sin category. Brief anonymous requests, one-tap "Praying for you" responses. Low friction, high intimacy.
